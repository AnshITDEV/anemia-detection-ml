About Anemia

Understanding Anemia

What you need to know about this common blood disorder

What is Anemia?

Anemia is a condition where your blood doesn't have enough healthy red blood cells or hemoglobin, reducing oxygen flow to your body.

Common Symptoms

Fatigue, weakness, pale skin, shortness of breath, dizziness, and cold hands and feet are typical signs of anemia.

Prevention & Treatment

Iron-rich diet, supplements, and proper medical care can effectively manage and treat most types of anemia.
